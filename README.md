## The Top 5 "Never Sign Unredlined" Clauses
1. **Uncapped Consequential Damages & Indemnification:** Always cap aggregate liability at `12 months of fees paid` (`1x Fees`).
2. **Pre-Existing IP Sweeps in Contractor Agreements:** Explicitly carve out `Pre-Existing Intellectual Property & Generic Developer Tooling` and condition IP transfer on `full payment of all invoices`.
3. **Evergreen Auto-Renewal with 60–90 Day Notice Windows:** Require written reminder notice from the vendor 30 days prior to the cancellation deadline.
